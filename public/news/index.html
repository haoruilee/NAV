What belongs on this calendar?

A publisher announcement is a dated document from an identifiable source. A historical valuation record is an eligible stored reference, not necessarily a new announcement. Neither label means the fund’s official NAV changed. Proposed financings and combined-entity values require separate model eligibility checks.

Dates refer to source publication or recorded valuation events. Retrieval times are not substituted for missing publication dates. Read the three-date guide.
