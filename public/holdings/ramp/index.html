How to interpret the weight

A hypothetical 10% portfolio allocation to a fund with a 7.11% recorded company weight contributes 0.711% of portfolio-level disclosed exposure. This arithmetic describes an indirect research exposure, not a direct security interest or a promise of redemption value. The look-through calculator combines selected fund allocations and preserves the unmapped slice.

Company-level financing valuations are not a security-by-security mark of each fund position. The selected source can be older than another headline because the model considers trust and eligibility before event date. Read excluded records and corporate actions on the source page before interpreting a company value.
