    The calculation, without the shortcut

    Issuer exposure = Σ(portfolio allocation to fund × disclosed issuer weight in that fund). Allocations must total 100%. Missing holdings remain unmapped; we do not inflate the known positions to make the total appear complete.

    Mixed reporting dates, class rights, fees and changes since the filing remain limitations. Dollar figures scale the illustrative portfolio only; they are not direct company-share entitlements.
