Methodology

A clearer view. With the evidence.

Independent tools and explanations for reading fund disclosures and company valuation signals.

What we publish

NAV Monitor brings dated fund NAV records, disclosed company holdings, source references and an explicitly labeled estimate into one research interface. It is not an issuer, broker, investment adviser or fund administrator.

How the work is produced

Research guides are original educational explanations published under the NAV Monitor organization name. A separate automated source-review process can propose records, while deterministic checks control whether records enter the dataset. We do not invent staff credentials, imply a human has reviewed every automated item, or represent an estimate as official NAV.

Corrections and accountability

Each numerical record should be read with its source and date. When comparing a discrepancy, distinguish the holdings baseline, the company event date and the audit check. A changed source or corporate-action term can require a new review rather than a silent numerical overwrite.
