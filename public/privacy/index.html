Methodology

Research without submitting a portfolio.

The current site and the optional advertising setup are described separately.

Calculator inputs

The look-through calculator runs locally in the browser. It does not submit entered allocations or illustrative amounts to our server. Changing calculator inputs does not write a portfolio to the account, database or public URL.

Serving the website

The web host and content delivery provider receive ordinary request information needed to serve pages and protect the service, which can include IP addresses, browser metadata and requested URLs. Linked external source websites have their own practices when you choose to visit them.

Advertising status

The homepage loads Google’s AdSense connection script for publisher ca-pub-7668037987581637. This connects your browser to Google, which receives request information and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. Analytics are not enabled. Manual advertising slots remain unconfigured and appear as placeholders. AdSense approval and any Google-managed automatic ads are separate publisher-account settings. If advertising is enabled later, third-party providers may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses or similar signals subject to the deployed consent settings and their policies. The advertising integration remains gated until a publisher account and the required consent setup are configured.

Future consent configuration

The technical integration does not send an ad request merely because an ad position exists. Enabling ads requires valid publisher and slot identifiers plus an explicit consent integration signal. Applicable Google-certified consent requirements must be addressed before serving ads to relevant regions. See Google’s publisher consent requirements and Google’s privacy information.
